All three platforms have global infrastructure and tools for managing cloud applications and services.
Business requirements influence cloud platform selection because companies need to choose services that match their budget, technology, security, and performance needs. For example, a company using Microsoft products may choose Azure, while a company focused on AI may choose GCP. The right cloud platform helps an organization meet its goals efficiently and cost-effectively.
